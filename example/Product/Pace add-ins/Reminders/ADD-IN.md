# Reminders

**Purpose:** Keep a dated follow-up in the record and, where the host can, set a timed reminder in its own app.

## When to use it

When the owner asks to be reminded or to follow something up, such as "remind me Friday to chase the auditor's letter", or when a reminder the host set opens a chat about a follow-up.

## What it needs

- What to follow up, the day it is due, who owes it and who it is for (the owner, unless they name someone else), and its project if it has one.
- A time, only if the owner wants a timed reminder.

## Steps

1. A reminder always has a day. If the owner gives none, ask which day. A time is not a date: "Friday at 2pm" is due on Friday, and the time belongs to the timed reminder.
2. Record it as a dated follow-up with what it is, who owes it, who it is for, the due day and the project, as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes. Never keep a reminder as an undated note, because it would never come up as due. Call it saved only after read-back.
3. If the owner wants a timed reminder, check whether this host has its own reminder or scheduled-task feature that can open this Pace folder when it goes off. A local scheduled task usually needs the computer on and the app open. A reminder that runs in the cloud or on a phone usually cannot open a local folder.
4. If the host can: set the reminder for the time asked, with a short instruction that names this Pace folder and the follow-up. Do not copy people's names, figures or other private details into the app. Say the reminder is set only once the app confirms it.
5. If the host cannot: say so in one line, for example "I can't set a timed reminder from here, so the follow-up is kept for Friday and will come up when you ask what is due." Say it is kept only if the read-back in step 2 worked.
6. When a reminder opens a chat: read the follow-up as it stands now, and say whether it is still open (and how late), moved to a new day, already done or cancelled, recorded two different ways, or not found.
7. To mark a follow-up done, moved or cancelled, record that change as a new record that points to the follow-up, as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes. Move or remove the app reminder in the app, or tell the owner where it is so they can.

## What it gives back

One or two lines: the follow-up and its day, whether it was saved, and whether a timed reminder was set, where and for when.

## Limits

- These steps apply only while handling this reminder. Nothing here carries over to the rest of the chat.
- A reminder sends nothing to anyone, and a reminder going off changes nothing in the record by itself.
- Never claim a timed reminder was set unless the app confirmed it, and never use a scheduler outside the host's own features.
