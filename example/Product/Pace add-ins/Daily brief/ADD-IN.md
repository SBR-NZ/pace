# Daily brief

**Purpose:** Gather what needs the owner today: decisions, overdue and upcoming work, and what is waiting on them.

## When to use it

When the owner asks for a daily brief, a morning catch-up or "what needs me today". It is a workflow the owner asks for, not a script to run at the start of every chat. A quick question about one item does not need it.

## What it needs

- Today's date and time zone. Use the host's clock where it has one; otherwise ask.
- Read access to `Record/` and `1 Projects/`.
- Optionally, the date of the last brief the owner kept, to show what changed since then.

## Steps

1. Say the date and time zone the brief is "as at".
2. Search the record for open dated work, decisions waiting on the owner, work others are waiting on the owner for, and anything recorded two different ways. Follow [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) for how to search and how to say what the search covered.
3. Sort what you found into these sections, leaving out any that are empty:
   - **Your decisions**: matters waiting on the owner's answer, unsettled ones first.
   - **Overdue**: oldest due date first, with the next step when one is recorded.
   - **Today and next**: work due today, then the next three weeks, earliest first.
   - **Waiting on you**: work someone else is waiting for from the owner.
   - **Still to clarify**: open work with no date, no person responsible, or two accounts, and any folder under `1 Projects/` that has no `Pace project.md` yet, with the question of what it is.
   - **Since the last brief**: work done since the date the owner gave, or, with no earlier brief, the last seven days with their dates.
4. Where the record holds two accounts of one item, show both together and ask which stands. Do not choose one. When the owner says which stands, correct the one that stands and withdraw the other, as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes.
5. Show anything missing as missing, for example "Date not recorded". Do not fill a gap with a guess.
6. Leave key numbers out unless the owner asks for them. If they do, use the Key numbers and changes add-in when it is switched on; otherwise say it is off.
7. Give the brief in chat. Open with the one thing that matters most, then the sections. If nothing needs a decision and nothing is overdue, say so in one line.
8. If the owner answers something in the brief, such as a decision, a new due date or which of two accounts stands, record it as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes. Call it saved only after it has been read back.

## What it gives back

A short brief in chat with its "as at" date, the sections above and one line saying what was searched and anything that could not be read. If the owner wants the brief kept, it is a dated view, not a record: see [Assistants and dated views](../../Help/hosts-and-views.md).

## Limits

- These steps apply only while preparing this brief. Nothing here carries over to the rest of the chat.
- The brief itself changes nothing in the record, and an item in it is not the owner's approval.
- A partial search is never called complete. If something could not be read, say so before the sections.
- It does not run on a timer by itself, and it sends nothing to anyone.
