# What was reported

**Purpose:** Keep the exact words of each reporting occasion, and answer what was reported, when and to whom.

## When to use it

When the owner wants a reporting occasion kept, such as "record what went in the March board pack", or asks what was told to an audience, such as "what did we tell the bank about cash in March?".

## What it needs

- To record an occasion: the exact words or figures as shown, the date, the audience, and the pack or file they were in when there is one.
- To answer: whatever narrows the search, such as the number, period, date, audience or file.

## Steps

### Recording an occasion

1. Take the wording exactly as shown, including its rounding and units. Do not tidy it.
2. Note the date and the audience. If either is unknown, record it as unknown rather than guessing.
3. If the wording came from a pack or file, keep that file as source evidence and note where in it the wording sits (page, slide, or sheet and cell). See [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md).
4. If the owner gave the words in chat with no file, record them as said, marked as from the conversation.
5. Mark whether it was actually reported or is still a draft. A draft stays "not confirmed as reported" until the owner says it went out.
6. Record it as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes, and call it saved only after read-back. A later occasion is a new record, even about the same number. A fix to how one occasion was captured is a correction of that record.

### Answering what was reported

1. Search the record for matching reporting occasions. Follow [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) for how to search and how to say what the search covered.
2. Answer from the exact words recorded for that occasion, with its date, audience and source. Never replace them with a newer actual or a more precise number.
3. If several occasions match, show each one separately. If the owner needs just one, ask them to narrow it by date, audience or period. Do not pick the newest.
4. Include drafts only when the owner asks for them, and label each one as a draft.
5. If a source file is missing or no longer matches what was recorded, say so and do not quote around it.

## What it gives back

When recording: what was recorded, and whether the read-back worked. When answering: each matching occasion with its exact words, date, audience and source, and a line saying what was searched.

## Limits

- These steps apply only while recording or answering this request. Nothing here carries over to the rest of the chat.
- Reported is not approved. A figure in a pack is not the owner's acceptance of it: see [Exact-act confirmations](../../Help/confirmations.md).
- It does not recalculate, convert units or add precision, and missing is never zero.
- It sends nothing to anyone.
