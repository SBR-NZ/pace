---
name: pace-what-was-reported
description: Launcher for the What was reported add-in in Pace by SBR. Use when the owner wants a reporting occasion kept, or asks what was reported, when and to whom.
---

This launcher only points to the add-in and adds no rules of its own. In the owner's Pace folder, open `Pace.md` and find the What was reported line in its add-in index. Open the file that line names, which is `Product/Pace add-ins/What was reported/ADD-IN.md` or the owner's customised copy, and follow it for this request. If What was reported is not in the index, it is off: say so, and switch it on only when the owner says to.
