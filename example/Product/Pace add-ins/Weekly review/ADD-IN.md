# Weekly review

**Purpose:** Go through open work with the owner, and record what is done, cancelled, given a date or owed by someone else.

## When to use it

When the owner asks for a weekly review, to go through or tidy up open work, or to add dates to work that has none. A question about one item does not need it, and nor does a plain list of open work: that is the Open work add-in.

## What it needs

- Read access to `Record/` and `1 Projects/`, and a way to save records, as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes.
- The owner's name as records write it, from the `Owner:` lines in `Pace.md`, so their own work is told apart from what others owe. If there are none, say so and offer to add one.
- The owner, there to answer. With no one there, such as on a schedule, only the list is made (see Limits).
- Optionally, one project to review, with the projects inside it, instead of every project.

## Steps

1. Say the "as at" date and time zone of the review.
2. Make the review list. **If you can run Python**, use the overview tool's review list, following "The overview tool" in [Assistants and dated views](../../Help/hosts-and-views.md). **Otherwise**, search the record for open actions as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes, follow each one to its current account, and sort them as below.
3. If something could not be read, say first that the list is partial.
4. Show the list in these groups, leaving out any that are empty. Number the items from 1 across the whole list, one line each: how late, how soon or how long open it is, its subject, its project, and who owes it when that is not the owner.
   - **Recorded two ways**: items with competing accounts. Show both accounts and ask which stands.
   - **Overdue**: the longest late first.
   - **Due today and in the next 7 days**: the earliest first.
   - **Waiting on others, with no due date**: work someone else owes, the oldest first. Offer to draft a chase; nothing is sent without the owner's own go-ahead.
   - **No due date**: the rest, the oldest first, with any that name no person.
   Then say how many items are due later and so are not in the list.
5. Go one group at a time, and no more than 15 items at once, so the owner can answer as they go.
6. Ask the owner what changes, by number. For each item they can say done, cancelled, a new due day, who owes it now or who it is for. An item they do not name stays as it is: never mark work done because it looks finished, is old or was skipped.
7. Before saving, show the changes as one list, such as "3. Board pack: done" or "12. Supplier call: due Friday 6 November 2026", and ask the owner to confirm. A day given in words, such as "Friday", is turned into a date and shown for the owner to check.
8. On the owner's yes in their own words, save each change as its own record that corrects the item's current account, with the owner's words, as [Records and source evidence](../../Help/records.md) describes; the record tool has a command for exactly this. Read each one back, call it saved only then, and say which, if any, could not be saved.
9. For an item recorded two ways, once the owner says which account stands, withdraw the other, then make any change the owner gave to the one that stands.
10. End with what changed: how many were done, cancelled, given a new date or passed to someone else, how many stay as they are, and anything not saved.

## What it gives back

The numbered list in chat, then the changes the owner chose, each saved and read back, and a short summary of what changed. The list itself is not a record; if the owner wants it kept, it is a dated view: see [Assistants and dated views](../../Help/hosts-and-views.md).

## Limits

- These steps apply only while doing this review. Nothing here carries over to the rest of the chat.
- Nothing is saved without the owner's yes in their own words. Silence about an item is not a change, and nothing is recorded for an item that stays as it is.
- With no one there to answer, such as a run on a schedule, make the list and stop: save nothing, mark nothing done and send nothing. The owner goes through it in a later chat.
- It sends nothing to anyone. A chase is a draft until the owner sends it or says to send it.
- A partial search is never called complete.
