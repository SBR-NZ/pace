---
name: pace-reminders
description: Launcher for the Reminders add-in in Pace by Simply Better Reports. Use when the owner asks to be reminded, or to follow something up on a given day.
---

This launcher only points to the add-in and adds no rules of its own. In the owner's Pace folder, open `Pace.md` and find the Reminders line in its add-in index. Open the file that line names, which is `Product/Pace add-ins/Reminders/ADD-IN.md` or the owner's customised copy, and follow it for this request. If Reminders is not in the index, it is off: say so, and switch it on only when the owner says to.
